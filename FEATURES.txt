NAVEE OFFLINE — UT5 MAX FEATURE AVAILABILITY
Validation date: October 12, 2026
Tested hardware: US UT5 MAX, model 2538; OEM protocol reference: NAVEE 2.1.9

The app talks directly to the scooter over Bluetooth. Optional map tiles use
internet; authentication, controls and recording require no server. First-time
owner setup is covered in SETUP.txt.

CONFIRMED WRITES AND FRESH READBACK

The parked scooter accepted a changed value, reported that new value, and
reported its original value after restoration for 25 routine settings, plus
lock/unlock. A changed charging-start timestamp was also read back and restored.
These checks prove configuration changes; they do not prove ridden behavior.
The choices below come from the OEM interface; the tests used representative
changed values, rather than every possible choice.

Area                  Confirmed configuration fields
--------------------  -------------------------------------------------------

                      startup threshold, maximum speed

Useful details:

- Recovery offers Off, Low, Medium and High. Stronger recovery changes coasting
  deceleration; its real energy benefit needs a ride measurement.
- Startup threshold offers 0–5 km/h. Maximum speed uses this model's OEM choices
  of 25, 32, 40 and 50 km/h; limits for other UT5 models are not offered.
- Charge ceiling offers 80%, 85%, 90%, 95% and 100%. Stored schedule settings
  were verified; actual charging start/stop behavior needs a charging cycle.
- Low Battery Mode limits maximum speed below 20% charge. It is not an Eco mode
  that reduces power throughout an entire ride.
- Lock duration offers 6, 12 or 24 hours of locked standby. The OEM says the
  scooter can remain locked after powering off; this is not a guaranteed timed
  unlock. Keep your configured handlebar PIN available.
- Walk Assist follows walking speed when pushed in Walk mode. Power Assist uses
  the Boost button and full throttle uphill in Sport mode. Hill Descent Control
  limits speed while descending without braking. These are OEM descriptions;
  propulsion and descent behavior still need a rider check.
- Line/star patterns offer Always On, Follow Headlight and Breathing. This
  model's normal and Boost colors are Light Blue and Red. Readback was verified;
  visual behavior has not been independently checked during this test run.
- This scooter currently exposes Theoretical, Drive and Sport range estimates.
  Changing the estimate does not change battery capacity or measured odometer.

STATS AND BATTERY DIAGNOSTICS

An earlier complete parked capture populated 83 of the 93 listed fields,
including response checks. A reconnect can initially supply fewer readings.
The app distinguishes missing and stale data instead of inventing zeroes.

Reported data includes:

- Charge, pack voltage/current, signed terminal power, temperature, reported
  health, charge counter, rated capacity and lifetime energy/capacity counters.
- Battery manufacturing date, deep-discharge events and temperature-related
  lifetime counters where the firmware supplies them.
- Speed, estimated remaining range, current trip, average/maximum speed,
  odometer and stored trips when driving/history reports arrive.
- Dashboard/controller/battery firmware, scooter identity, warning/status
  fields, raw non-secret diagnostic payloads and each reading's age.

Reported health and health-adjusted capacity are not independent battery tests.
Lifetime Wh/Ah counters are not current-ride consumption. Power is calculated
from paired voltage/current readings. Ride energy needs fresh samples and a
confirmed current direction before it can be called net battery consumption.

No decoded per-cell voltages, cell balance, motor/controller temperature,
torque, direct per-trip Wh or exact remaining pack Wh were found. Unknown fields
retain honest raw labels; firmware faults are not silently cleared.

Lab controls require an exposed status field, an understood original value,
fresh stationary data and a matching readback after an accepted command.
Missing or unexplained baseline values keep controls unavailable. The app has
no arbitrary command console, ownership reset or firmware-write feature.

APP TOOLS AND REMAINING VALIDATION

The app includes local profiles, appearance/text/unit choices, fold-aware
layouts, charts, ride recording, notes, comparison and exports. More uses saved
App, Scooters, Security, Lab and About sections, showing one at a time without
rebuilding their controls. First-time owner setup stays visible when needed.

Keep idle connection in background defaults off. When off with no recording,
hiding the app or turning the screen off starts a 10-second grace period. If
still hidden without a recording, the deadline closes the idle session, cancels
reconnects and removes its notification. Failed connections and retries cannot
extend it.
Returning to the app cancels the deadline. Changing the option while already
hidden or stopping a hidden recording creates no new grace period. Active
recording continues independently with its notification until stopped. No lock
state changes automatically.

All five tabs were rendered in Android at the Pixel's outer-screen size.
Expanded and rotated half-fold Home layouts were checked using controlled
window/hinge callbacks while the phone was folded/asleep; physical hinge
transitions remain untested.

Two parked background recordings (60 and 20 seconds) saved with no journal
gaps. Longer recordings, road-range measurements and Ollie's phone remain
untested. CSV/JSON export logic passed; the Android document picker was not
physically exercised overnight. See VERIFICATION.txt for the exact scope.

Ordinary diagnostics exclude owner identity and PIN digits. A private owner
backup intentionally contains the setup identity and must stay private.
Cloud activation, ownership transfer, account sharing, firmware hosting and
Find My/Find Hub networks remain outside this offline app.


0.5.0 PHONE RECORDING AND INTERFACE

Six destinations: Home, Scooter, Lights, Stats, Rides and More. Settings use compact
groups and value rows; all headlight, line and star controls are under Lights.

New explicit recordings can save GPS, pressure and experimental motion separately
from electrical-energy sampling. Route preview colors power, grade and GPS speed
derivative; tap for synchronized readings. Phone raw, linear and earth-frame
acceleration are retained in CSV when the relevant sensors are available.

Grade and ascent are estimates with signal-quality gates. GPS and Bluetooth gaps
remain gaps. No inferred accelerometer distance is used. Older rides do not gain
GPS. Pocket IMU quality and outdoor GPS/pressure need physical ride validation.

Optional interactive OpenStreetMap tiles have caching and linked attribution,
with a replaceable HTTPS source. Route capture/drawing work without a map server.
