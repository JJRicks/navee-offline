0.6.1 — DASHBOARD EDITING REFINEMENT

Editing now uses a pencil entry, a dedicated top-left grip and a visible
48 dp bottom-right corner hit target. Direct dragging and long pressing share
the same interaction. A full-size dashed destination previews the snapped
rectangle; neighboring widgets reflow and compact into gaps from an unchanged
gesture-start snapshot. Release saves; cancellation restores the complete
original layout. Neighbor movement animates for 200 ms and the destination for
120 ms when Android animations are enabled. Measurement probes keep the active
column layout stable; an actual width change cancels and restores the gesture. One ellipsis opens options; tapping
the grip or corner opens only Move or Size respectively. Scooter controls stay
inert during editing. Theme-matched dialogs fix title/input/button contrast.

The battery ring uses % and fits its number/unit group inside the arc. The
scooter card has an explicit rename pencil and configurable defaults for total
odometer, current mode, reported charge count and lifetime Ah throughput.
UT5 MAX PID 2538 modes 11/2/3 display WALK/ECO/SPORT based on owner observations;
other models/codes remain raw. Parsing and exports retain numeric evidence.
Existing card placements are kept; a fresh scooter card is taller for its stats.

SearchFocus binds only search fields in Scooter/Lights, Stats and the widget
parameter picker. Outside taps clear focus/hide the keyboard while passing the
touch through. Keyboard visibility observations clear focus after system IME
dismissal. Ordinary rename, title and other form fields are not opted in.

HISTORICAL 0.6.0 — CUSTOM DASHBOARD AND CONNECTED HISTORY

Home is a per-scooter dashboard with fifteen widget types, independent column
layouts, native drag/drop, resize handles and explicit Size/Move alternatives.
Controls are disabled while editing. The default battery card includes estimated
range, battery power, reported health and temperature. Gauge scales are stored in
canonical units and converted for display. A local scooter name appears on Home,
and the supplied UT5 MAX image appears when that model is connected. Metadata
was removed from the bundled photo without changing its compressed pixels/alpha.

The five destinations are Home, Scooter, Stats, Rides and More. All ten lighting
controls are nested under Scooter > Lights. The former Headlight label was wrong:
OEM source identifies command 0x54 and status 0x70 byte 4 as TailLight. Automatic
lights (0x57, byte 8) is separate. The app reports configuration readback rather
than claiming a lamp visibly changed; no independent front-headlight command was
inferred or added.

Last-known parameter/settings readings persist per scooter and display grey with
stale context after disconnect/restart. Live authentication and freshness gates
still control writes. Automatic connected-only history does not start a ride,
phone sensors or GPS, and does not extend background connection life. Its 50 MiB
budget uses capped SQLite storage and retention/pressure pruning: up to 7 days of
detailed readings, 30 days of minute aggregates and 730 days of daily aggregates.
Reported battery health remains a controller report, not a measured capacity.

The battery card replaces ordinary readings with charging power/current and a
present-rate time estimate only when fresh state reports charging. Otherwise it
returns to range/power/health/temperature. A separate Charging widget is optional.
Paired V/I, rated Ah and fresh percentage are required for the applicable values;
charge cutoffs and taper limitations are visible. Live charging display was
observed on the owner's Pixel; the time-to-full prediction has not been compared
with a completed charge.

The capacity experiment compares recent qualified ride energy with displayed
battery-percentage drop, using separate confirmed-net and unconfirmed-throughput
pools. This is an experimental energy equivalent, not an independent capacity or
health test. It updates on ten-second viewed refreshes, supports a selectable ride
window and editable advertised Wh, and uses 716.04 Wh only for UT5 MAX PID 2538.
Active ride snapshots are atomic; completed journals replay outside the recorder
monitor. Tests cover quality rejection, separate pools, weighting, active-ride
replacement, direction changes, read-only snapshots and source/profile isolation.
Charging tests cover state/freshness/pairing, polarity, current thresholds,
invalid signals, charge limits and present-rate estimates.

HISTORICAL 0.5.0 — PHONE RECORDING, MAPS AND GROUPED SETTINGS

The interface now has six destinations: Home, Scooter, Lights, Stats, Rides and
More. Scooter settings use grouped rows and detail dialogs; all ten headlight,
line-light and star-light controls share the Lights destination. More has a
settings index, and measurement source details/favourites open on tap in Stats.
The prominent lock control and automatic connection behavior remain available.

Optional phone recording adds GPS routes, pressure-based relative elevation,
smoothed grade estimates, speed-derived acceleration and experimental motion
samples. A separate phone journal keeps these samples out of the existing
scooter electrical integration. Route points can be inspected alongside fresh
scooter speed, power and battery readings. CSV, JSON and GPX exports preserve
accepted fixes and route gaps. Pocket motion is explicitly distinguished from
forward scooter acceleration; missing/low-quality measurements are not replaced
with invented values.

Route geometry renders locally. Optional OpenStreetMap street tiles load only
when Street map is enabled; a configurable HTTPS tile provider and local cache
are supported. Version 0.5.0 therefore requests INTERNET permission for maps.
Connection, authentication, lock/settings and recording do not require a server
login or the tile provider. Historical no-INTERNET statements below describe
older APKs, not 0.5.0.

0.4.0 NAVIGATION AND FUNCTIONALITY PASS

Home retains the prominent lock control. Other tabs have a compact lock and
connection header with feedback. Controls has seven sections and search across
all settings. Stats separates Overview, Readings, Charts and Diagnostics, with
search/favourites first in Readings. More has App, Scooters, Security, Lab and
About. Sections are remembered; tapping the selected main tab returns to its top.
Ride comparison is available beside selected recordings, and CSV/JSON export
is at the top of ride details. Partial-direction energy is explicitly labelled.

Background connection OFF uses one fixed ten-second grace from leaving the
foreground. Returning cancels it. Failed connections, retries and late callbacks
cannot extend the deadline. At expiration, BLE and reconnect work, service
polling and the notification stop. Active recordings and background ON are
explicit exceptions. Cold hidden startup receives no grace; ending an exception
while already hidden shuts down without creating another grace period.

Interrupted export preparation is cleared after process restart without
interrupting an active copy worker during Activity recreation. Slider CANCEL
and closed screens discard drafts; only successful release submits a setting.
Discovery cancels an earlier connecting session and each new scan replaces its
old timeout. Rejected selections/PIN requests retain their dialog and feedback.
The core protocol and recorded energy math are unchanged in this pass.

The historical control, battery and physical fold evidence below describes the
0.3.0 tests. The 0.4.0 runtime checks passed on the Pixel: all five tabs, all 7 Controls /
4 Stats / 5 More sections, search/no-match/clear, favourites and native slider
DOWN/MOVE/CANCEL while disconnected. Navigation and cancellation submitted
zero scooter mutations. Four native View renders were inspected on the outer
display; these are native app renders, not physical fold movement tests.

Lifecycle checks passed: full ten-second grace, resume cancellation, a fresh

No recordings or scooter mutations were created. A separate real Android Home

IMPLEMENTED SCOPE (HISTORICAL 0.3.0 BASELINE)

   Five native tabs: Home, Controls, Stats, Rides and More. Consistent cards,
   charts and navigation; dark/light/system themes; text-size and unit choices;
   large lock control with accessibility state. All five compact layouts were
   rendered and inspected using Android's actual app View tree.

2. Local scooter controls
   The OEM's understood local controls are mapped with model-specific choices,
   availability checks and confirmation evidence. 25 routine settings were
   changed to a representative alternative and restored with fresh readback.
   Lock/unlock and a scheduled-charging timestamp were separately changed and
   restored. Features and limitations are listed in FEATURES.txt.
   PIN/proximity setup is implemented; the owner's existing PIN and Bluetooth
   bond were not changed overnight. No firmware write or ownership-reset UI.

3. Diagnostics
   93 catalogued fields with source, age, missing/stale labels and read-only raw
   payloads. An earlier complete capture populated 83 fields; some reports are
   conditional and are not returned in every connection. Live voltage/current,
   terminal power, battery details, odometer and range estimates are included.
   Sensitive owner-auth and PIN traffic is excluded from ordinary diagnostics.

4. Ride and energy recording
   Private persistent sessions, elapsed/moving/stationary time, coverage and
   connection gaps, paired electrical samples, notes, history, comparison,
   charts and CSV/JSON exports. Interrupted journals recover without inventing
   missing measurements. Unconfirmed current direction is labelled as absolute
   electrical throughput; net consumption is not claimed without confirmation.
   Background recording was exercised while the phone was asleep, including
   a logical outer-to-inner display transition. Actual riding is still needed.

5. Folding phones
   Compact cover-screen navigation, expanded navigation rail, vertical hinge
   spacing and a horizontal half-fold layout. BLE is owned by the service so
   layout changes do not recreate the scooter connection.
   SDK-controlled display-state and rotation checks used the Pixel's real OEM
   window-layout callbacks. Native renders were inspected at 1080x2364 outer,
   2076x2152 inner and 2152x2076 rotated half-open sizes. These were app View
   renders while the phone was physically folded/asleep, not physical hinge
   movements or camera/display photographs. Ollie's phone was not available.

6. First-time setup and profiles
   In-app owner setup guidance, bounded validated text/file import, private
   owner backup, profile switching and saved scooter identity. The ADB helper
   captures a fresh owner-auth frame from an already activated OEM setup;
   it validates the frame and restores temporary logging settings.
   Parser fixtures and isolated Android profile migration/forget/backup tests
   passed. The existing real owner setup was not replaced for a test.
   Each friend needs their own scooter's identity. ADB is currently needed for
   the initial capture, not subsequent connection, controls or diagnostics.

7. Launch and lock
   Cold launch selects Home. Automatic connection to the saved scooter is
   enabled by default. The prominent lock control submits directly without a
   confirmation dialog. The displayed position follows confirmed fresh state;
   motion/freshness checks guard locking. Opening the app never changes lock
   state automatically. The BLE lock/unlock cycle was confirmed and restored.

8. Connection reliability and efficiency
   Serialized BLE setup and writes, setup callback deduplication, bounded
   queues, serial/profile validation, reconnect backoff, command deadlines and
   fresh readback. Uncertain writes are not replayed on reconnect. Heavy screen
   refreshes are limited to once per second; connection/lock status updates
   immediately. Battery polling is adjustable, idle polling slows, and the
   wake lock is used only for active recording. The idle foreground service
   stopped after testing with background connection disabled.

9. Testing boundaries
   Internet settings were not changed. Reversible settings were restored;
   the scooter finished unlocked with no active test recording. No cloud
   account changes, ownership transfer, reset or firmware changes were made.
   Logical device-state and rotation overrides were returned to the original
   physical/default state after fold checks. The separate validation APK is
   removed after final checks; it is not a production app component.

10. Package
    Same Android package and signing key as the prior app; installation retains
    private settings. Minimum Android 8, target Android 16. The APK signature
    and alignment checks passed. The manifest requests no INTERNET permission.
    Source/build/test/setup files are included; owner identities, PIN digits,
