Questions

Good to know.

Is Volt made by NAVEE?

No. Volt is an independent project and isn't affiliated with NAVEE or its app vendor.

Which scooters does it work with?

Volt is built for the NAVEE UT5 MAX (US, model 2538) that you already own and have activated. Other models, regions and firmware may behave differently.

Does Volt need the internet?

No. Locking, controls, stats and ride recording all work offline. The optional map background is the only feature that downloads anything.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. Volt runs on Android 8.0 and newer, including foldables.

Is Volt open source?

Yes. Volt is MIT-licensed and developed in the open on GitHub. Every release comes with a signed APK and SHA-256 checksums.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. You can export rides, or make a password-protected backup of everything whenever you like.
